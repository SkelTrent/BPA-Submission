The idea

Why one city at a time?

If you want to get to know a city, start with what its people make: the bakery with a line out the door, the spice blend a family has sold for years, the honey from the hills just outside town.

Marlowby gathers a few of those things into one box, so you can get a feel for a place before you ever visit.

Read our mission

  1. Made by locals

    Everything in the box comes from small, independent businesses in that month’s city.

  2. Chosen by people who live there

    A local curator helps us decide what goes in each box.

  3. A story with every item

    The field guide explains who made each item, and why it matters to the city.
